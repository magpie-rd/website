About Us

Magpie R&D offers a full range of engineering services.

Magpie R&D is an independent engineering consultancy supporting early-stage innovation in complex physical systems. We combine mechanical design, sensor system architecture, and algorithm development to help startups and inventors rapidly test ideas, build proof-of-concept prototypes, and reduce technical uncertainty.

Our work focuses on technologies where hardware and data meet — from intelligent medical devices to defense sensing platforms and agricultural monitoring systems.

We help teams learn faster, make better technical decisions, and move confidently toward product development.
