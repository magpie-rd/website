Services

Expertise that drives innovation

Magpie R&D offers engineering services tailored to R&D-focused teams, startups, labs, and inventors. Our services are rooted in deep technical expertise, a passion for creative problem solving, and a hands-on, data-driven approach to product development.

Mechanical Design & Prototyping

Transform your ideas into functional hardware. I support the full design cycle—from concept to manufacture-ready CAD.

  • 3D CAD modeling and 2D drawing creation
  • Prototyping and mechanical fixture design
  • Design for manufacturability and testability
  • Experimental design validation (thermal, fluidic, stress analysis)
  • Simulation (FEA, CFD, Optics) development and review

Data Analysis & Algorithm Development

From raw data to actionable insights in biomedical and wearable applications.

  • Sensor fusion and time-series analysis (e.g., IMU, PPG, ECG)
  • Signal filtering, feature extraction, and algorithm tuning
  • Simulation and modeling of biological or physical systems
  • Image processing and workflow automation
  • Custom data analysis tools and scripts
  • MATLAB, Python, LabVIEW, C++

Test System & Fixture Design

Design test setups that deliver meaningful data for performance validation.

  • Custom mechanical, electrical, and optical test fixture design
  • Test plans and verification protocols
  • Sensor selection and DAQ system design
  • Automation of test workflows
  • Documentation and test result interpretation

Feasibility Studies & R&D Strategy

Clarify ideas and reduce risk early in development.

  • Feasibility studies and design-of-experiment planning
  • Competitive technology scouting
  • Technical concept evaluation
  • Support for early-stage grants or proposals

Cross-Functional Engineering Support

Helping teams connect the dots between hardware, software, and business goals.

  • Engineering documentation and design reviews
  • Requirements definition and risk management
  • Collaboration with mechanical, firmware, UX, and regulatory teams
  • Stakeholder updates and technical reporting

Let’s bring your idea to life. Whether you’re building a prototype or validating a product, reach out to discuss your project or request a free consultation.
